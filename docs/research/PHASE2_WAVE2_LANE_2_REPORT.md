## Top 3 Picks

1. **doorstop-dev/doorstop** - most mature governance/release shape for requirements-heavy adoption (changelog, workflows, release lineage, lock-based Python build).
2. **upstash/context7** - strong fit for agent-doc accuracy use case with good repository hygiene (security policy, workflows, lockfile, active release cadence).
3. **mem0ai/mem0** - high functional upside for memory-layer capability with active maintenance and test surface; best treated as controlled pilot first.

## Top 3 Avoids

1. **GongRzhe/Office-Visio-MCP-Server** - highly niche plus minimal quality/release/security controls visible.
2. **SensAI-PT/RAGMeUp** - useful concept, but insufficient release/test/security governance evidence for reliable adoption.
3. **opactorai/Claudable** - sparse operational hygiene signals (no workflows/changelog/security policy) relative to production expectations.

## Notes

- This review is repository-surface based and intentionally conservative where CI state or policy evidence was unavailable.
- `tests_status` remains `unknown` unless explicit, current pass/fail evidence was directly observable from checked artifacts.
