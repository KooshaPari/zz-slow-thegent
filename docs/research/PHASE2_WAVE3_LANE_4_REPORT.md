## Top 2 Picks

1. `Nordlys-Labs/nordlys` - strongest engineering hygiene (CI matrix, package structure, explicit security policy, reproducible workflows).
2. `jehna/humanify` - narrowly scoped but operationally mature utility with clear release/version discipline.

## Top 2 Avoids

1. `ChrisRoyse/Pheromind` - strategy narrative dominates; implementation and governance artifacts are insufficient for adoption.
2. `ruvnet/agentic-security` - promising ideas but high automation and supply-chain risk surface with weaker release/security governance than required for safe adoption.
