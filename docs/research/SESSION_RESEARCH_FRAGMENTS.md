## 2. Pareto Routing & Hysteresis (WP-1004 / WP-5001)

- **Concept**: Route 80% of low-risk tasks to efficient "Lifecycle" loop; 20% high-risk to "The Gent" (Plan/Operator/Reviewer).
- **Hysteresis**: Use a damping band (e.g. 5-minute dwell time) when switching between normal and protective scale modes to avoid "thrashing" concurrency caps.

## 3. Economic Governance (WP-5003)

- **Concept**: Agent decisions weighted by cost-to-value ratio.
- **Implementation**: `CostAwareRouter` in `catalog.py`. Uses provider scoring (reliability, latency, cost).

## 4. MAIF Action Artifacts (WP-3002)

- **Concept**: Signed artifacts for every significant agent action.
- **Storage**: Persistence in Supermemory L4. Hash chains for verification.

## 5. Simulation & Sandbox (WP-4007)

- **Implementation**: `ux/replay.py`. Uses Supermemory L3 to retrieve past decision context.
