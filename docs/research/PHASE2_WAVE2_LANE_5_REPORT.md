## Top 3 Picks

1. `browser-use/browser-use` - strongest near-term utility for browser-agent execution, with active releases and clear CI/test/security posture.
2. `getzep/graphiti` - best memory/knowledge-graph substrate with high engineering maturity and release hygiene.
3. `searxng/searxng` - robust self-hosted retrieval/search backbone with mature security/integration workflows.

## Top 3 Avoids

1. `OthersideAI/self-operating-computer` - minimal test/security/release hygiene relative to autonomy/safety risk profile.
2. `bar181/openai-agents` - education-oriented content, not production-grade implementation surface.
3. `sdi2200262/agentic-project-management` - promising framework but comparatively thin CI/test/security maturity for adoption now.
